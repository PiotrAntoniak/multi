   native model" immediately after a table whose MultiEURLEX row is 0.426 vs 0.357 (+19.6%
   relative, fail at 0.975). The sentence is true for FLORES only.
2. **+0.069 is computed from rounded values.** Exact delta is +0.069853 -> 0.070, not 0.069. FLORES

3. **The FLORES pass is thin in cell coverage.** The 24-cell mean passes (1.005), but only

   (`imbalanced_recall_mel.py:7`), but v1 ran on CUDA (`device=cuda`, v1 log line 1) and v2 on CPU

6. **The v2 `train_pos` column is English-only.** `imbalanced_recall_mel_v2.md` reports
   `train_pos=108` for concept 13, which is the EN count. The DE native model trains on 86

7. **E15 omits `n_rare=8`.** The table says "rare concepts" without the count. The base script's

   The claim rests on docstrings, the v1 log, and the simulation in Verified item 9. Direction is
   corroborated; the original v1 means cannot be checked.

### Bugs / risks

1. **Tie handling makes FPR@R90 optimistic.** `_cum_curves` (`imbalanced_recall.py:148-154`) sorts
   with a stable argsort and `fpr_for_recall` takes the exact position of the 90%-recall positive.
   Within a group of tied scores this acts as an oracle tie-breaker. Under threshold semantics
   (all tied items share the prediction), the `agg`/education-and-society cells move
   **0.9242 -> 1.000** (6/24 FLORES cells, max shift 0.0758). Checked mel cells show no material
   shift (0.0000). The FLORES mean changes only slightly because both arms shift together, but
   individual cell values are affected.
2. **No uncertainty.** Single seed, single split, no bootstrap or confidence intervals. FLORES
   cells have 4-20 positives (sent) and 4-7 positives (7 positives total for agg education), with
   132-135 negatives in agg (FPR resolution ~1/132). A one-row change can move a cell by 0.19-0.25.
3. **Downstream contamination in E16 (`mel_close`).** `mel_close.py:186-189` reads only
   `en_train.jsonl` / `en_test.jsonl` labels and trains its "native" ceiling on DE/FR features with
   EN labels — the same bug as v1. Its native ceiling mean AP is **0.0674** versus **0.3826** from
   corrected v2 native models (5.7x higher). `mel_close.md:31` also points to the deleted
   `imbalanced_recall_mel.md`, and its rare-concept table (`mel_close.md:35-50`) reproduces the
   buggy native values (e.g. 13/de AP 0.014, FPR@R90 0.969). E16's "gap closed 699.8%" and the
   E9/E10/E11 claims built on that ceiling are therefore at risk. Out of E15 scope, flagged here.
4. **Unweighted cell means + saturated cells.** Every rare concept x language counts equally.
   The `agg` education cells (6/24) are uninformative (both models must take all 7 positives) and
   still move the FLORES mean.
5. **512-token truncation.** MultiEURLEX mean documents are 6.7k-13k characters but embeddings see
   only the first 512 tokens. "Document-level" rare-concept detection is really head-of-document
   detection; this is inherited from the recipe but not stated in E15.
6. **The base script can overwrite the published FLORES headline.** `python imbalanced_recall.py
   --datasets sent,agg,mel` would replace `imbalanced_recall.json/.md` with a 4-concept mel block
   (`:658-682`) and rewrite `imbalanced_pr_mel.png`; outputs are unversioned. Default
   `--datasets sent,agg` is safe, but the safe path is undocumented.
7. **The mel driver cannot regenerate v1.** `VERSION = "v2"` is hardcoded
   (`imbalanced_recall_mel.py:65`). This prevents accidental overwrite but also means the v1 arm is
   permanently unreproducible, contradicting the create-only narrative.
8. **Environment warning.** sklearn emits `A NumPy version >=1.23.5 and <2.5.0 is required ...
   (detected 2.5.4)`. Runs completed and reproduced exactly, but this combination is untested
   upstream and could break on a fresh environment.
9. **Orphaned status/PID files.** `imbalanced_recall_mel_status.json` and `.pid` describe a
   completed run whose outputs no longer exist; any status-based watcher would report success for
   missing files.
