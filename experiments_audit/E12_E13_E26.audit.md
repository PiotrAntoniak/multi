### 3. Method

- **Global shared PCA** (`dim_removal.py:183-195`): fit on the stacked-language train matrix,
  project out the leading component, re-fit on the residual, repeat. Verified numerically on
  FLORES-agg 210m: `V5[:3] == V3` exactly; orthonormal to 1.6e-15; projector idempotent to 3e-8;
  k=5 removes 26.6% of total energy.
- **INLP** (`dim_removal.py:198-208` -> `backbone_grid.py:452-484`): language-identity logistic
  probe on the stacked train pool, iterated null-space projection, cap set to k. Not always 5
  directions: mel 210m returns 2 (reported as `dirs=2` in `dim_removal.md:95`).
- **Random control** (`dim_removal.py:211-216`): Gaussian QR, seed 0, 5 orthonormal directions;
  stored only at k=5.
- **Model** (`dim_removal.py:93-94, 243-253`; `backbone_grid.py:555-638`): fixed XGBoost 400 trees,
  depth 8, lr 0.1, max_bin 64, n_jobs 2, seed 0; multiclass/binary/21-label; EN train -> EN test and
  each target test.
- **XL-mean macro-F1** (`dim_removal.py:256-261`): unweighted mean of per-target macro-F1. Confirmed.
- **EN-self** is the same model on EN test; in-language is target train -> target test, PC series,
  mel skipped (`dim_removal.py:305-316, 339`).
- **0.975 bar: N/A.** The artifact has no target/English ratio and no gap-closure metric. The only
  ratios are the auditor's derived XL/EN and gap-closure values in the companion `E12_E13_E26.md`.

## Discrepancies

1. **"EN-self flat" is overgeneralized** (`EXPERIMENTS.md:163`). It is flat for mel (range
   0.015-0.022) and toxic (0.014-0.019), but FLORES-agg 210m spans 0.457-0.568 (range **0.111**)
   and FLORES-sent 210m spans 0.376-0.428 (0.052). At the selected best k the change is <=0.045,
   so "flat at the optimum" is defensible; "flat" across k is not.
2. **"Random control stays below" is false for toxicity** (`EXPERIMENTS.md:163`). `toxic/210m`
   random k=5 is 0.470 vs PC best 0.467; `toxic/610m` is 0.5012 vs PC best 0.5005. It is also above
   PC k=5 for `flores_sent/210m` (0.201 vs 0.178), though below the PC peak (0.259). The claim should
   be "below the PC peak on the four topic cells; at/above it on toxi_clean".
3. **The table omits the 610m FLORES rows and all four extras.** The JSON/MD have them; the section
   table does not. The omitted extras include the largest effect (`sib200` +0.201) and a toxicity
   counterexample (`textdetox` +0.063).
4. **"Neutral/harmful for toxicity" is `toxi_clean`-specific.** `textdetox` (binary toxicity,
   `dim_removal.md:197-210`) gains +0.063 at k=3 (0.591 -> 0.654) and is classified
   "language-specific" in the MD. The section finding should name the dataset, not the task family.
   Likewise, xnli (+0.104) and pawsx (+0.029) gain, so "helps topic tasks" is too narrow.
5. **`aggregate.en_pc.n_dirs` is wrong/unstable in the JSON.** `collect` (`dim_removal.py:372-375`)
   overwrites `n_dirs` for every k unit, so the final value is whichever unit was iterated last.
   `mel/210m` reports `n_dirs=4` while the series has k=0..5 and the k=5 unit has `n_dirs=5`.
   The MD does not print this field, so the visible tables are unaffected.
6. **The summary header "safe k (F1<=-0.02)" is inverted/misleading** (`dim_removal.py:474-475`);
   it should say "safe k (XL-F1 >= k0 - 0.02)". The adjacent "drop" column is also `f5 - f0`, so a
   positive value is a gain, not a drop. Separately, `_safe_k` (`dim_removal.py:381-393`) breaks at
   the first k below tolerance, so a non-monotonic series is under-reported: `textdetox` gets safe
   k=0, although k=2 (0.596), k=3 (0.654), k=4 (0.651) and k=5 (0.586) are all within or above the
   0.591 - 0.02 = 0.571 band. No headline row is affected.
7. **E13 and E26 have no separate evidence.** They appear only in the merged heading
   (`EXPERIMENTS.md:150`). No E13/E26-specific script, table or claim exists; attribution across the
   three IDs cannot be reconstructed.
8. **The "96-98% language-aligned" claim is second-hand.** `dim_removal.md:7` cites E1b; the E1b
   script/artifact is missing (`COVERAGE.md:45`). The functional claim is supported by this audit's
   probe (language eta^2 collapses at k=3), but the exact 96-98% number cannot be re-verified from
   this artifact.
9. **"Best k" is selected on the test metric.** `render` (`dim_removal.py:512`) takes `argmax` of the
   test XL-F1 over six k values. The "best XL-F1" column is therefore an oracle maximum; the doc
   presents it as a result without this qualification. With 139 FLORES-agg test rows and a single
   seed this is material.
10. **The k=0 baseline is not the project's best raw baseline.** E28's tuned raw XGB is 0.294 on
    FLORES-agg 210m (`backbone_grid.json`, cell `flores_agg|210m|mean`, depth 6, lr 0.024, gamma 0.69)
    vs this experiment's 0.204. E28's tuned model is far more regularized; part of the small-k gain
    here may be regularization by projection. E21 uses the `perlang_z` transform (same XGB
    params); E14 uses raw features but depth 6, 200 trees. Their k=0 numbers are not comparable.

## Bugs/risks

1. **Stale checkpoint reuse (confirmed; highest severity).** The log starts with
   `checkpoint hits=20, to run=109`. The 20 hits are t400_l0 cells written `23:01-23:08`, before
   `dim_removal.py` was last modified at `23:13:11`. Affected groups:
   - `flores_sent/210m`: INLP 23:01:50, random 23:01:49, inlang it/de/fr 23:03:08-23:04:09.
   - `flores_sent/610m`: INLP 23:03:32, random 23:03:55, inlang it/de/fr 23:06:15-23:07:43.
   - `flores_agg/210m`: INLP 23:06:33, random 23:06:36, inlang it/de/fr 23:06:57-23:07:19.
   - `flores_agg/610m`: INLP 23:07:25, random 23:07:30, inlang it/de/fr 23:08:05-23:08:16.

   The FLORES INLP k=5 control does not reproduce with current code:
   `flores_sent/210m` stored 0.246901 vs fresh 0.236437 (Delta 0.0105; EN-self 0.369312 vs 0.381082).
   The random cells reproduce exactly (checked for `flores_sent/210m`); the 12 in-language cells are
   also pre-revision but were not re-run. The MD's `inlp@k5` control column is affected at the
   0.01 level. The "language-specific" classification itself compares the PC gain to the random gain,
   so it is not directly affected; the INLP mismatch is a control-value error.
2. **Checkpoint signature is not a code signature.** `_run_sig` (`dim_removal.py:230-232`) is only
   `t{trees}_l{row_limit}`. It ignores the script version, XGB params other than trees, the PCA/INLP
   code and the feature pipeline. Any change silently reuses old cells. This is the root cause of B1.
3. **Smoke/subset runs clobber the published artifacts.** `run_all` always writes
   `dim_removal.json/.md` and all 8 PNGs (`dim_removal.py:655-661`), and `build_plan` only contains
   the selected datasets. The docstring's `--smoke --no-extras` "plumbing check" will overwrite the
   400-tree full-run evidence with 60-tree/800-row results; `--datasets mel` will overwrite the JSON
   with a single dataset. There is no `--out-prefix`, no smoke suffix, and no guard.
4. **INLP k=5 is cap-dependent and not nested.** `inlp_dirs` sets `BG.INLP_MAX_DIRS = K`, runs the
   iterated projection, then SVDs the accumulated directions and returns `basis[:K]`
   (`dim_removal.py:202-207`, `backbone_grid.py:478-484`). The first 5 directions of a 30-direction
   INLP basis and a 5-direction fit are nearly orthogonal: principal angles **45.4-89.9 degrees**
   (max coordinate difference 0.356). Fresh XL-F1 is 0.2364 (cap 5) vs 0.2318 (cap 30, first 5).
   "The k=5 language subspace" is therefore one arbitrary variant, not a stable object. E1b's
   30-direction result is not a prefix of it.
5. **`_safe_k` early break** (B/D6): a later recovery is ignored. Fix by scanning all k and taking
   the largest k that satisfies the tolerance.
6. **Random control is single-seed and only k=5.** The doc compares the PC peak at k=2/3 to random
   at k=5, which is asymmetric and overstates specificity. Fresh random k=2 on FLORES-agg 210m is
   0.257 vs PC k=2 0.266 (almost all of the k=2 gain is generic); random k=3 is 0.241 vs PC k=3
   0.380 (the k=3 peak is genuinely above random). Multiple seeds at the same k are needed.
7. **INLP control is not a fixed 5 directions.** mel 210m returns 2 directions, so the "INLP at k=5"
   column mixes a 2-d and a 5-d control across cells. The MD discloses `dirs=2`, but the summary
   column does not.
8. **PCA stack is corpus-size weighted.** For toxic, EN has 24000 train rows and DE 9836; PCA
   maximizes total variance, so the leading directions are dominated by the larger languages. The
   "global shared PCA" is not language-balanced. A per-language-equal weighting or a per-language
   centering before the stack would test this.
9. **No threshold calibration.** Binary and 21-label macro-F1 use a fixed 0.5 threshold
   (`backbone_grid.py:620-630`). The topic gains are corroborated by AUC, but the F1 magnitudes (and
   the "neutral/harmful" calls) are threshold-dependent. A quantile-matched or validation-tuned
   threshold would make the F1 comparison fairer.
10. **Memory cost.** `pc_dirs` converts each language block to float64 and vstacks all languages on
    every iteration (`dim_removal.py:185-194`). For toxic 610m the stack is ~96.7k x 1152 float64
    (~0.89 GB) per call, repeated six times per cell; the actual run used 4 workers (`log` line 1),
    so peak memory is multi-GB. A rerun on a smaller machine can OOM.
11. **Extras are not reproducible from the repo alone.** The 60 cached `.npy` files live in
    `%TEMP%\opencode\more_datasets\emb_cache`; the HF datasets cache is also required. The JSON
    `config` records only `emb="emb_bf16"`, not this temp path or the caps used.
12. **Environment warning.** Current interpreter: numpy 2.5.4, scipy 1.15.3, sklearn 1.6.1,
    xgboost 3.2.0; importing xgboost warns that SciPy expects numpy <2.5.0. The fits succeeded, but
    the environment is not pinned in the artifact.
13. **Single seed, no CIs.** All cells are seed 0; the FLORES-agg test is 139 rows with rare classes.
    Differences of 0.01-0.02 (including the INLP control shifts) are not resolvable.
14. **Provenance gaps in the config.** The JSON does not record the actual command, `--workers`
    (log says 4; default is 3), script/PCA/INLP versions, or a git hash. The run is resumable but not
    bit-reproducible from the artifact alone.

## Recommended changes

1. **Invalidate the 20 pre-revision checkpoints** (delete or move the FLORES INLP/random/inlang
   t400_l0 cells) and re-run the FLORES cells; or at minimum recompute the four INLP controls and
   update `dim_removal.json/.md`. Add a `code_hash` (script + imported module hashes) and the full
   XGB param dict to `_run_sig`.
2. **Namespace outputs**: write `dim_removal_smoke.*` / `dim_removal_<datasets>.*`, or refuse to
   overwrite full-run artifacts when `--smoke` or a dataset subset is active without `--force`.
   Document that `--smoke` is destructive as written.
3. **Fix `collect`**: set `en_pc.n_dirs` to `K_MAX` (or the max k present), not the last unit's
   value (`dim_removal.py:372-375`).
4. **Fix `_safe_k`**: scan all k, do not `break` at the first failure; correct the summary header to
   "safe k (XL-F1 drop <= 0.02)".
5. **Add fair controls**: random directions at k=2,3,5 with at least 3 seeds; report the random
   value at the same k as the PC peak, and the mean +/- range. This is the single most important
   scientific fix: the current random-k=5 comparison exaggerates specificity at small k.
6. **Make INLP nested**: fit the full 30-direction INLP once and evaluate prefixes of a fixed
   direction order, or report the sequential projection subspace. Do not let the cap change the
   reported k=5 basis. Add a principal-angle check between caps to the artifact.
7. **Report a validation-selected k** using the existing `Xva` split, alongside the test-selected
   "best k", and label the latter as oracle-selected. Add bootstrap CIs over test rows (or 3 seeds).
8. **Report test sizes and class counts** in the MD (505/139 for FLORES; 21-label mel; toxic
   per-language test sizes). Flag FLORES-agg's rare classes.
9. **State the unlabeled target-train feature use** in the caveats (it is in the header but easy to
   miss), and add a per-language-balanced PCA variant for toxic, or at least disclose the imbalance.
10. **Add threshold calibration** (quantile-matched target thresholds, as in E18's mel protocol) or
    make AUC the primary metric for mel/toxic; keep the 0.5-threshold F1 as a secondary number.
11. **Fold the extras into the section**: the 610m FLORES rows and the four extras belong in the
    E12/E13/E26 table, or the table should state that it is a 4-dataset excerpt. In particular,
    `textdetox` contradicts the task-level toxicity claim and `sib200`/`xnli` show the effect is not
    topic-specific.
12. **Add a language-probe/eta^2 check to the artifact** (1.000 -> ~0.44 after k=3; eta^2 collapse)
    so the "language-aligned" claim rests on this run rather than on the missing E1b.
13. **Record the run command, workers, env versions and a git/script hash** in `config`; keep the
    extras cache path and caps there too.
